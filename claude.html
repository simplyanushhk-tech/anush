About Claude AI

Claude AI is an artificial intelligence assistant developed by Anthropic. It is designed to help people with learning, writing, coding, research, summarizing information, and answering questions. Claude AI can understand natural language and provide clear, accurate, and helpful responses on many topics.

Visit the official website:Claude AI

Usefulness:
• Helps students complete assignments and understand concepts.
• Assists writers in creating emails, stories, and articles.
• Supports programmers by explaining code and finding errors.
• Summarizes long documents into short, easy-to-read points.
• Answers questions quickly and helps with research and learning.
